How [Brand Name 2] cut cost per lead and booked more qualified calls

Pairing high-intent Google Search with Meta lead generation, and measuring quality, not just lead volume.

The Client

[Brand Name 2] is a [short description of the business, service and ideal client]. Their sales depended on booked consultations, so lead quality mattered more than raw lead count.

Ads were running on a single platform with a generic offer, and there was no feedback loop between ad performance and what sales actually closed.

The Challenge

Leads were coming in, but a large share were unqualified or never replied. Cost per lead kept rising as the audience fatigued on the same creative. No tracking beyond form fills, so quality by campaign was invisible.

The Solution

Google Search captured people already looking for the service, while Meta created demand with lead forms and retargeting. Offline outcomes were fed back so campaigns optimized toward quality.

Organic social built trust before the sales conversation: education and proof, planned around the questions prospects actually ask.
