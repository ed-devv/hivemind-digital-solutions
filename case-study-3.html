The Client

[Brand Name 3] is a [short description of the business, services and service area]. Customers search before they buy, but the business was largely invisible on search.

The site had technical issues, thin service pages and no content targeting the questions local customers ask.

The Challenge

The site did not rank for the core service + location keywords. Technical issues (speed, indexing, page structure) were holding pages back. No content or Google Business Profile strategy to capture local intent.

The Solution

SEO was the long game; Google Ads covered high-intent searches in the meantime, and the paid keyword data helped decide which terms deserved organic investment.

Social supported search: it carried local proof and helped reinforce the brand for people who found the business on Google.
